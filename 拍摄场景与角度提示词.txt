通用摄影规则
每张只拍一个场景×一个角度。构图和机位优先于展示完整环境；特写只让环境在背景局部或虚化可见，正俯视只露少量环境边缘。首张产品图只锁定产品结构、花型及面料，不锁定背景、光线或机位；不得水平翻转成左右视图。中性白平衡，花型底色、纹样与印花中的文字保持不变；禁止新增文字、水印和拼图。检查场景特征、取景范围、A/B面、件数及物理结构。
ONE photograph for exactly this scene-angle pair, never a collage, contact sheet or split view. The selected camera geometry and crop take priority over showing all scene anchors: close-ups retain only a partial softly blurred contextual background, overhead retains only narrow set edges; retain contextual medium-close detail scale and editable margins without turning the detail into a full-room shot. Lens, height, tilt and frame coverage are approximate photographic guides, not visible annotations. Preserve neutral white balance on the textile, original base fabric color, motif identity and any lettering already printed in the uploaded fabric; no added captions, logos or watermarks. Never horizontally mirror a print to simulate another viewpoint. Before finalizing, check visible scene identity where the crop allows it, camera position, crop scale, product count, physically coherent folds and exact A/B assignment. If a rule conflicts, retain uploaded pattern/color identity and product construction first, requested camera composition next, environment decor last.

9 个拍摄场景

儿童床品 [childbed]
儿童专用单人床、圆角低矮家具、少量绘本和木质玩具；所有产品在儿童床上展示，保留所选花型工艺。
实际提示词：
children bedding photography in a bright child bedroom, realistic single child bed 90-120cm wide and 180-200cm long, low rounded frame, child-height bookshelf, restrained pastel accents and a few wooden toys in background. All selected products use this child bed as support, preserve uploaded fabric print and selected craft. No adult double bed, crib, people or invented cartoon print.

暖白侘寂 [warm]
暖白灰泥墙、低矮床架、亚麻窗帘，家具少而克制。侧窗柔光、柔和接触阴影，暖感来自米白环境，面料保持原色。
实际提示词：
warm ivory wabi-sabi bedroom. Required visual anchors: textured ivory plaster wall, low understated bed frame, oatmeal linen curtains; sparse furniture and quiet organic shapes. Soft lateral window daylight creates gentle contact shadows. Warmth comes from ivory surroundings, not a yellow filter. No ornate mouldings, dark hotel wall, coastal view or busy decorations.

原木自然 [natural]
浅橡木床头与床架、原木床头柜，少量绿植；柔和晨间侧光。保留自然木纹，避免与纯白简约或侘寂灰泥场景混淆。
实际提示词：
natural oak bedroom. Required visual anchors: clearly visible pale oak headboard and bed frame with real wood grain, matching simple timber bedside table, one restrained green plant. Soft morning side-window daylight, neutral fabric white balance, pale calm surroundings. Oak is the defining background material; no hotel walnut wall, ornate French carvings or seamless studio backdrop.

雅致酒店 [hotel]
深胡桃木床头、左右床头柜、成对壁灯和克制酒店陈设；均衡漫射照明。呈现真实酒店空间，不添加酒店常见的多余枕头或床尾巾。
实际提示词：
elegant dark walnut hotel bedroom. Required visual anchors: substantial dark walnut headboard wall, paired bedside tables and paired restrained reading sconces, tidy upscale hotel architecture. Balanced diffuse room lighting with a soft window key light, readable bedding shadows and no dark crushed fabric. Hotel luxury must come from furniture, not extra pillows, bed runners or altered bedding. No oak cottage, garden or plain studio.

纯色影棚 [studio]
无缝米白纯色背景和简洁摄影承托床台，柔光箱均匀布光，保留真实阴影。画面没有家居陈设、窗户、绿植及海景。
实际提示词：
professional clean ivory photography studio. Required visual anchors: seamless solid ivory cyclorama background and a minimal neutral bed platform or product support; background has no domestic wall features. Large diffused softbox lighting on both sides, subtle real grounding shadows, controlled highlights and neutral fabric color. No bedroom furniture, windows, curtains, plants, scenery or decorative props. Photographic three-dimensional product, not a cutout or flat illustration.

温暖户外 [outdoor]
花园露台或遮棚木平台上的真实床架，背景可见绿植和开放空气；柔和午后日光。床品保持干燥洁净，无橙色滤镜、野餐毯或草地铺床。
实际提示词：
warm outdoor garden terrace. Required visual anchors: a real bed frame on a dry sheltered wooden deck, visible garden greenery beyond and open-air space without an enclosing bedroom wall. Soft late-afternoon daylight under light shade; warmth in surroundings with neutral fabric colors, no orange cast. Keep bedding clean, dry and supported. No picnic blanket, fabric on grass, tent, rain, bare outdoor mattress or indoor bedroom masquerading as outdoor.

海景露台 [terrace]
有护栏的浅石材海景露台，远处可辨海面和地平线，真实床架承托床品；通透漫射日光，无蓝色染色或热带沙滩陈设。
实际提示词：
airy coastal sea-view terrace. Required visual anchors: pale stone terrace floor, safe balcony railing and a visibly distant sea with a level horizon; a real supported bed within a sheltered open-air terrace. Airy diffused daylight and restrained coastal neutrals, neutral fabric white balance without blue tint. The sea is background, never a print on the textile. No garden-only scene, beach sand, tropical props or hotel interior without an actual sea view.

现代简约 [minimal]
光滑白墙、直线床架、几何床头柜、整洁现代建筑线条，中性日光。区别于粗糙侘寂墙、原木房间和无缝影棚。
实际提示词：
minimalist contemporary white bedroom. Required visual anchors: smooth white architectural wall, straight-lined simple bed frame, geometric bedside table and clean restrained room lines. Balanced neutral window daylight with soft realistic shadows. Clearly a habitable modern bedroom, not a studio cyclorama. No textured wabi-sabi plaster, carved French furniture, dark hotel panelling, dominant wood grain or clutter.

法式卧室 [french]
奶油色墙面线框、克制雕花床头、法式床头柜和长窗帘，柔和窗光。法式特色来自房间，不额外改造床品、添加蕾丝花型。
实际提示词：
elegant French bedroom. Required visual anchors: cream wall-panel mouldings, a restrained carved curved headboard, a small French-style bedside table and long soft curtains. Gentle neutral window daylight, delicate depth and natural contact shadows. French character belongs to the room only: do not add lace, ruffles, embroidery or a new floral print to the selected bedding. No gilded palace excess, modern square headboard or studio backdrop.

童趣房间 [kids]
浅彩墙面、圆角家具、少量玩具或无文字童趣墙饰，明亮柔和自然光。童趣来自环境，保持用户花型及产品比例，不添加儿童、卡通床品或多余抱枕。
实际提示词：
bright playful children bedroom. Required visual anchors: restrained pastel wall, rounded safe furniture and one small shelf toy or simple non-lettered playful wall decoration. Bright soft natural daylight, tidy room with uncluttered product display. Playfulness comes from the setting, never from replacing the uploaded print with cartoons. Keep selected product dimensions and component count, no people, extra decorative pillows, crib conversion or crowded toy pile.

实际提示词：
45-degree three-quarter full-product photograph. Bed coordinate system: stand at the foot facing the headboard; left and right are the viewer's left and right. Camera is at the foot-left diagonal, about 45 degrees from the bed longitudinal axis, lens roughly 0.6 m above the mattress, tilted down about 25 degrees. Use a normal 40-55 mm full-frame equivalent lens. Show the complete product including foot and one side, product occupies about 60-70% of frame with margins. No straight front, pure lateral profile, overhead or wide room shot.

正面全景 [front]

Headboard horizontal, bed centered, left and right edges balanced and nearly symmetrical in perspective. Show all selected product components and lower edges, product about 60-70% of frame. No diagonal three-quarter camera, lateral profile, overhead or wide room view; natural fabric need not be mechanically symmetrical.

俯视平铺 [top]

Product long edges remain parallel in image space, complete product footprint within frame and about 60-70% coverage with comfortable editing margins. Bedding rests naturally on a real horizontal support with plausible creases and contact shadows, not an unsupported texture swatch. No visible vertical front of headboard, eye-level room view, diagonal camera or perspective convergence. Include a restrained contextual edge of the selected setting.

面料特写 [detail]
中近景拍主体面料，细节约占画面50%～65%，保留周围连续布面和修图空间；清楚看到真实织纹、花型边缘和柔和褶皱，小段缝线可选，不把整床或枕套当主体。

Show sharp real fibers, undistorted motif edges and one gentle shallow fold; a small real seam may be visible but is not mandatory. Moderate depth of field so the principal texture remains readable, background softly blurred. No full bed, room overview, pillow-specific composition unless the product itself is pillowcases; do not invent coarse fibers or embossed print.

低机位 [low]
从床尾略偏侧，镜头接近床面下方约10～20厘米，水平或轻微仰拍；突出边缘厚度、侧垂边和柔软接触阴影，不拍成俯视全景，不夸张广角变形。
实际提示词：
low camera angle at mattress height. Camera near the foot slightly to one side, lens about 10-20 cm below the mattress top, horizontal or at most 5 degrees upward, 35-50 mm equivalent lens without wide-angle stretching. Make the product front edge, thickness and side contour prominent, retain enough upper fabric and headboard for orientation; product about 60-70% of frame. Show realistic weight, compressed contact edges and material-appropriate folds. No high downward viewpoint, overhead, heroic extreme perspective, invented thick mat or floor-level inaccessible underside.

空间远景 [wide]
从房间门口或场景边缘的斜前方拍摄，机位约1.3～1.5米；整床占画面约40%～50%，完整床架、地面和环境可见，床品花型仍可辨，不让装饰遮挡。
实际提示词：

Complete bed including frame occupies about 40-50% of frame; show floor and distinct setting anchors around it while print remains legible. No foreground obstruction, cropped bed feet, tiny distant bed, macro texture or product-filling close framing. For studio use wide studio context, not an invented bedroom.
