# 凉席拍摄角度更新

选中凉席时，拍摄角度切换为凉席专用示例；未选具体类别时展示冰丝席示例。选择冰丝席、藤席或竹席后，12 张缩略图、悬停大图和发送给图像服务的当前角度参考同步切换。返回四件套时恢复四件套图，保留已勾选的角度。

三套图均为 1448 × 1086，4 列 × 3 行；每格 362 × 362，不拉伸。完整产品视图包含 1 张薄席和 2 只匹配的席枕套，特写可自然裁出枕套。自上传拍摄参考图优先于自动角度参考；示例中的颜色、花型和房间不替代用户的 A/B 花型及所选场景。

| 位置 | 角度 | 展示内容 |
|---|---|---|
| 第 1 行第 1 格 | 45° 斜侧 | 完整凉席及席枕套 |
| 第 1 行第 2 格 | 正面全景 | 床尾正对床头的完整产品 |
| 第 1 行第 3 格 | 俯视平铺 | 垂直俯视的席面轮廓 |
| 第 1 行第 4 格 | 席面特写 | 编织或竹片与细窄包边 |
| 第 2 行第 1 格 | 左侧全景 | 左侧机位的薄平席面 |
| 第 2 行第 2 格 | 右侧全景 | 右侧机位重新拍摄 |
| 第 2 行第 3 格 | 低机位 | 席边厚度与床垫承托关系 |
| 第 2 行第 4 格 | 空间远景 | 完整床架和周围环境 |
| 第 3 行第 1 格 | 席底展示 | 局部轻抬露出席底；竹片只在连接缝处转动 |
| 第 3 行第 2 格 | 席面床尾 | 下半席面、完整床尾包边 |
| 第 3 行第 3 格 | 席枕特写 | 匹配的席枕套与包边 |
| 第 3 行第 4 格 | 席边包边 | 平放在床垫顶面的侧边结构 |

## 冰丝席示例图提示词

参考：四件套角度图仅用于布局、拍摄距离；原凉席参考图左上格仅用于冰丝席结构。

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Create a new photorealistic bedding e-commerce CAMERA ANGLE ATLAS for an ICE-SILK SUMMER SLEEPING MAT (冰丝凉席). Input image 1 is a layout and photographic camera-distance reference ONLY (it shows a duvet set; replace the duvet completely). Input image 2 shows three mat materials; its TOP-LEFT panel is the ICE-SILK product structure reference ONLY. Output a landscape canvas with EXACT 4:3 aspect ratio, ideally 1448 x 1086 pixels, containing exactly TWELVE equal SQUARE photographs in a seamless 4 columns x 3 rows grid, no gaps, borders, text, labels, numbers, watermarks or montage within any individual tile. Each tile is independently a full single photograph. Same real 1.8m wooden double bed, quiet cream bedroom, daylight, neutral ivory ice-silk sleeping mat with tiny subdued sage leaf print, visible fine tight woven mesh, narrow sage woven binding; exactly two ordinary rectangular LOW-PROFILE pillows in matching ice-silk mat pillowcases at the head. Mat lies THIN and FLAT across mattress top, edge ends flush at mattress edge. Absolutely no quilt, duvet, bedspread, blanket, puffy batting, ruffles, hanging sides, or downward textile drape. The white mattress side remains visible. Attractive natural real photography, recognizable physically realistic mat weave. In full views step camera back so entire mat and BOTH pillows remain within image with 12-18% breathing room and mat occupies about 60-70% frame; wide room view mat occupies 40-50%. Medium details show enough surroundings (not extreme macro). Exact row-major tile order: row 1 col 1 = 45-degree side diagonal full bed view; row 1 col 2 = front symmetrical full mat view from foot; row 1 col 3 = strict 90-degree vertical overhead entire rectangular mat and pillow pair; row 1 col 4 = medium-close fine woven mat texture and bound seam in lower corner. Row 2 col 1 = LEFT true side profile of entire bed with thin mat upper surface; row 2 col 2 = RIGHT true side profile, clearly opposite side; row 2 col 3 = low camera near mattress height looking along thin mat with BOTH pillows head-end and entire mat corners visible; row 2 col 4 = wide environmental bedroom including entire bed small in composition. Row 3 col 1 = reverse-side display: only one small corner of soft ice-silk mat gently lifted on mattress to show thin underside and bound edge, main mat stays flat and both pillows visible; row 3 col 2 = foot-end lower-half view of thin flat mat full foot width and bound foot corners, context mattress side; row 3 col 3 = close-medium view of the matching rectangular ice-silk mat pillowcase pair, low-profile and woven, no duvet; row 3 col 4 = medium long SIDE BOUND EDGE display: thin mat edge resting flush on mattress top with at least half long-side edge visible and naturally connected top surface, NOT hanging blanket. Keep photograph crops complete, square and coherent.
```

## 藤席示例图提示词

编辑目标：冰丝席角度图。保留各格机位与环境，替换席面、包边和席枕套。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this 4 columns x 3 rows camera-angle atlas. KEEP exact canvas 4:3 aspect ratio (1448x1086), 12 equal square cells and each photograph's camera angle, crop, composition, room, daylight, wooden bed, visible white mattress side and contact shadows. Replace ONLY ice-silk sleeping mat and the TWO matching low-profile mat-covered pillows in EVERY CELL with a real natural RATTAN SLEEPING MAT (藤席): light honey-tan tight over-under flat rattan ribbon basket weave, clearly actual flat woven strips with small natural variation, narrow sand-beige woven binding, restrained satin-matte natural fibers, NO leaf print, NO flowers. Exactly one thin FLAT mattress-top mat and TWO rectangular woven rattan pillowcases, not a duvet set. Main mat stays level and ends flush at mattress top, no hanging sides. Row3col1 underside reveal must be only a small restrained BROAD curved corner of real woven rattan with visible thin underside and narrow binding, NOT a sharp folded duvet flap, not soft fabric wrinkles; rest stays mattress-supported. Preserve row1col4 weave/corner detail, row3col2 foot binding, row3col3 pair of mat pillows, row3col4 long supported bound-side edge. Full views show entire product with spare framing and both pillows, pillow tops and mat corners not cut. Row2col3 retain LOW lens at mattress height; row2col4 retain WIDE environmental full bed. No new objects, no blanket, quilt, bedspread, puffy padding, extra pillow, ruffles, logos, text, labels, borders or gaps. All photographs must consistently depict the SAME rattan mat and two rattan pillowcases, photoreal catalog quality. Every cell square, no deformation.
```

## 竹席示例图提示词

编辑目标：冰丝席角度图。竹片保持硬挺，席底展示只在竹片连接缝处转动。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied sleeping-mat CAMERA ANGLE ATLAS. KEEP exact 4:3 landscape canvas (1448x1086), seamless 4 columns x 3 rows of twelve equal SQUARE photos, original camera position/crop of each tile, room, wooden bed, daylight, white mattress sides, two rectangular pillow positions. Replace ONLY all ice-silk mats and both matching mat pillowcases with a natural BAMBOO SLAT SLEEPING MAT (竹席): parallel small smooth honey-brown hard bamboo SLATS across bed width, regularly spaced cream longitudinal stitching strings connecting strips, fine real bamboo grain, slight restrained sheen, thin brown woven tape binding, NO printed leaves/flowers. Thin bamboo mat lies entirely FLAT on top of mattress, no duvet, quilt, blanket, sheet drape, puffy padding or hanging edges. Each low rectangular sleeping pillow must have an appropriately low-profile bamboo mat pillowcase with visible small rigid slats connected by stitching and bound edges; exactly TWO in complete bed views. Material stays rigid: no cloth folds or textile wrinkles on bamboo. IMPORTANT ROW3COL1 reverse-side tile: replace lifted soft triangular corner with a modest straight JOINT-FLEX display of several rows of bamboo slats at foot corner, lifting only along connecting seam hinges to show thin woven backing beneath; rigid strips themselves remain STRAIGHT and unbent and most mat stays mattress-supported. No pointy bend crossing hard bamboo slats. Other tile order invariant: row1 45degree diagonal, frontal, true90degreeoverhead, medium corner/texture; row2 left lateral, right lateral, low mattress-height, wide room; row3 small reverse joint lift, lower-half foot-boundedge, close-medium matching mat pillowcases, supported longside boundedge. Weave close-up replaced with clear bamboo slat and string detail; keep each main seam/edge complete and detail medium with context. Full views step camera back to keep all mat corners and TWO pillow tops in frame with 12-18% editing space; wide bedroom fullbed view. No extra cushions, decor change, text, watermark, labels, frames, borders, gutters, tile count change. Photorealistic commercial bamboo mat product photography.
```
